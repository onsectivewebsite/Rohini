Meet Your Nutritionist

Dt. Rohini Sachdeva

Dt. Rohini Sachdeva is a highly qualified and compassionate nutritionist who has been transforming lives since 2015. With a deep passion for holistic health and science-backed nutrition, she has helped over 20,000 clients across India and 25+ countries achieve their health and wellness goals.

Her approach is never one-size-fits-all. Every client receives a fully personalised diet plan that considers their medical history, lifestyle, cultural food preferences, and specific health goals — creating sustainable results without deprivation.

She runs in-person clinics in Batala and Amritsar and provides online consultations worldwide via video call and WhatsApp.
